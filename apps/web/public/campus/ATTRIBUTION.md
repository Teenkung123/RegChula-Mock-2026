Attribution and licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . navigation-data.json is the derived walking graph; model-sources.json records original source identifiers and checksums.

MHMK and TAB building/room facts: Chulalongkorn University Faculty of Science BBTech building guide, https://www.bbtech.sc.chula.ac.th/building/ (checked 2026-10-18). No reference photographs or proprietary meshes are bundled.

The supplied v3 model's source/reuse notes and manifest accompany the GLB. Roofs, facades, heights and bridge elevations are schematic. The route overlay uses horizontal network coordinates and is not a surveyed elevation or indoor route. Routing endpoints are nearby mapped path nodes, not verified doors; gates and closures are not monitored.
